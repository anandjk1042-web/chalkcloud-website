Wholesome, Satisfying Gameplay

A charming fusion of classic drop-and-merge puzzle mechanics with cozy animations, juicy haptics, and whimsical themes.

🎈

Tactile Drop & Merge

Drop bouncy balloons and watch them merge into higher tiers. Feel the satisfying bounce and cheerful celebrations as your collection grows.

🎨

Charming Theme Albums

Collect Coins and Stars to unlock delightful balloon themes: Classic Sprout, Luscious Fruits, Sweet Candies, and Adorable Animals.

🌟

Daily Challenges & Perks

Solve curated daily puzzles, earn streak bonuses, and use Balloon Poppers to keep your puzzle jar from overflowing.

🔒

Private & Account-Free

No account sign-ups, no tracking of personal info, and no passwords. Your progress stays right where it belongs: on your personal device.
